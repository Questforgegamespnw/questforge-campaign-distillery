This stage takes the approved campaign identity and develops it into a concrete, playable premise. The concept material now defines:

- the starting situation;
- the central conflict;
- why the crisis is happening now;
- the players’ initial position;
- the major factions or forces in tension;
- the recurring campaign engine;
- how the situation may escalate;
- the meaningful choices available to the group;
- the central campaign hook.

- which elements feel strongest;
- anything that should be changed, reduced, or removed;
- any setting or system decisions that should now be treated as final;
- any remaining questions or concerns.
