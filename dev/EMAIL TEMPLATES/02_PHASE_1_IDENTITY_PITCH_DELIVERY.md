# Phase 1 Identity Pitch Delivery

**Suggested subject:** Your QuestForge Identity Pitches are ready for review

---

Hello {{client_name}},

Thank you again for sharing your campaign preferences with QuestForge Games PNW.

Attached are three **Phase 1 Identity Pitches** for your review:

- **Primary**
- **Adjacent**
- **Wildcard**

These are broad creative directions rather than finished campaign concepts. Their purpose is to help identify:

- what the campaign is fundamentally about;
- what kind of play it should emphasize;
- the tone and emotional promise that best fit the group;
- which overall direction feels most exciting and appropriate.

The pitches are intentionally **system-agnostic and setting-agnostic** at this stage. They do not yet define a specific established setting, central crisis, faction structure, starting situation, or recurring campaign premise.

Please review all three directions and let me know which one resonates with you most.

When replying, it is helpful to include:

- the direction you prefer;
- any specific elements you especially liked;
- anything you would like reduced, avoided, or changed;
- whether you already have a preferred game system or setting;
- any additional thoughts that should guide the next stage.

Your selected Identity Pitch will become the foundation for **Phase 2 Campaign Concept Development**, where the broad direction will be turned into a concrete, playable campaign premise.

There is no need to evaluate the pitches based on how complete they feel as settings or plots. At this stage, the most important question is simply:

**Which direction feels most like the campaign your group would want to play?**
